AI in Healthcare

Artificial Intelligence is being used in hospitals to help doctors and improve patient care.

How is AI used?

  1. AI can help doctors analyse X-rays.
  2. AI can help identify signs of diseases.
  3. AI can help monitor patients.

Benefits to Society (in short)

AI can support faster diagnosis and help healthcare professionals make decisions.
Faster & More Accurate Diagnostics: Image-recognition and computer-vision tools spot abnormalities (such as cancer, strokes, or fractures) in scans earlier and with high precision.

Reduced Clinician Burnout: Ambient AI scribes, automated documentation, and administrative tools free up millions of working hours for doctors and nurses to focus on direct patient care
