Video Assistant Referee

A match official in association football who assists the referee by reviewing decisions using video footage and providing advice to the referee based on those reviews.

4 categories of decisions that can be reviewed:

  1. Goal/no goal - attacking team commits an offence, ball out of play, ball entering goal, offside, handball, offences and encroachment during penalty kicks.
  2. Penalty/no penalty – attacking team commits an offence, ball out of play, location of offence, incorrect awarding, offence not penalised.
  3. Direct red card – denial of obvious goal-scoring opportunity, serious foul play, vilient conduct/biting/spitting, using offensive/insulting/abusive language or gestures.
  4. Mistaken identity in awarding a red or yellow card.
